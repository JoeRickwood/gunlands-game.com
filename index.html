About

Gunlands is an fps action roguelike about crafting weapons and fighting an evil cult ready to overthrow the world with an army of ascended mind-controlled soliders.

Find gun parts, assemble them into weapons of mass destruction, and let the chaos rain down upon your enemies.

Features

Randomized Dungeons

Procedural dungeons that learn from player builds and provides apt challenge that will be engaging for every player

Limitless Gun Crafting

Millions of combinations of effects on gun parts means that no one run is the same, every gun piece is generated and found within the dungeon

Faced Paced Combat

Intense combat inspired by the likes of Ultrakill and Doom, focused on speed and movement with powerful feeling weapons
